## Realistic roadmap

1. Before private pilots: independent correction-intent review, more diverse blind semantic evaluation, creator/reviewer walkthroughs, provider retention review and isolated per-customer accounts.
2. Reliability and operations: bounded asynchronous review jobs, usage quotas/rate limiting, background expiry alerts with delivery monitoring, migrations, backups and restore drills.
3. Evidence quality: richer source conflict semantics, paraphrase-aware retrieval and labelled retrieval-recall tests. Retain visible provenance and abstention.
4. Enterprise/visual scope only after validation: SSO, RLS, retention/deletion controls, private object storage, broader image analysis and authorised integrations. Automatic publishing remains outside the product promise.

No dates, adoption counts, guaranteed accuracy or cost savings are promised without evidence.
